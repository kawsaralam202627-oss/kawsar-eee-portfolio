A LITTLE ABOUT ME

More about my journey.

I’m Kawsar Alam, an Electrical and Electronic Engineering student who enjoys understanding how systems work—from circuit-level details to larger electrical networks.

MY STORY

Curiosity is where it starts.

My goal is to build strong fundamentals and gradually turn what I learn in class into practical, well-documented work.

At university, I’m developing my understanding of electrical machines, electronic devices and circuits, measurements, simulation, power systems and technical communication. I value patience, teamwork and learning from experiments that do not work on the first try.

This website will grow with me: new projects, lab reports, skills and milestones will be added as I gain experience.
